What it intentionally excludes

This page does not calculate age, payroll deadlines, or business days. Holiday dates can be overlaid, but weekends are not excluded and holiday rules are not interpreted as legal or contractual deadlines.

Formula and methodDate-only values are normalized to UTC before subtraction so daylight-saving transitions do not create misleading 23-hour or 25-hour calendar days.
Total days
|End UTC − Start UTC| ÷ 86,400,000
Inclusive days
Total days + 1
Complete weeks
floor(Total days ÷ 7), with Total days mod 7 remaining
Calendar duration
Complete years → complete months → remaining days
Worked example: 2 August to 3 August is 1 day apart. When both dates are included, the same selection contains 2 calendar dates. Months and years are calculated from actual calendar boundaries rather than fixed 30-day or 365-day estimates.
Tips and common mistakesClear counting rules matter more than extra fields. These checks help users apply the result correctly in personal, academic, administrative, and specialist settings.

Useful tips

  • Confirm whether the final date should be counted.
  • Use total days when comparing exact durations.
  • Use years, months and days when explaining the period to people.
  • Check the date format before entering dates manually.
  • Use the report when the interval supports a specialist record.
  • Use the Holidays tab to document relevant dates, but keep working-day rules separate.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming every month has 30 days.
  • Counting both dates unintentionally.
  • Confusing days apart with calendar dates included.
  • Ignoring leap years.
  • Entering the day and month in the wrong order.
  • Treating calendar days as working days.
  • Treating holiday dates as automatically verified legal deadlines.

Method and guidance

Calculation Guide

Formula, how to read the result, practical tips, common mistakes, FAQs, references, and limitations.

Formula and method

Total days = |End UTC − Start UTC| ÷ 86,400,000
Inclusive days = Total days + 1

Calendar years and months are added using real calendar boundaries; remaining whole days complete the human-readable duration.

How to read the result

  • Use total days for exact comparison.
  • Use years, months, and days for explanation.
  • Check whether the end date is included.
  • Read the status before using a future milestone.
  • Keep working-day rules separate from the included holiday overlay.

Tips

  • Confirm the governing counting rule.
  • Check the entered dates before saving.
  • Use the report for a clear audit trail.
  • Verify automatic and manual holiday dates against the governing calendar.
  • Record whether the result is inclusive.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming 30-day months
  • Counting both dates unintentionally
  • Confusing calendar and working days
  • Ignoring leap years
  • Using date-only output for time zones

Quick FAQs

Is the end date included?Only when inclusive counting is selected.
Are leap years included?Yes, when February 29 falls inside the interval.
Can dates be reversed?Yes. The direction is preserved and the distance remains accurate.
Are working days calculated?No. Holidays can be identified, but weekends are not excluded.
