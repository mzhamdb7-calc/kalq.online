Measure Height Correctly

Stand barefoot against a wall, look straight ahead and measure to the top of the head. Small height errors can noticeably change BMI.

Use a Consistent Weight

For progress tracking, use the same scale at a similar time of day and under similar clothing conditions.

Add Waist Context

Waist-to-height ratio can complement BMI. Measure the waist consistently and use the ratio as an additional screening indicator.

Interpret the Result Carefully

BMI does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, pregnancy-related changes or overall health. Consider professional advice when the result matters clinically.

Metric Formula

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres²

US Customary Formula

BMI = weight in pounds × 703 ÷ height in inches²

Healthy Weight Conversion

Weight = selected BMI boundary × height in metres²

BMI Prime

BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25. A value of 1.00 corresponds to BMI 25.

What does BMI measure?

BMI compares weight with height. It is a screening measure used to group adult weight ranges, but it does not directly measure body fat.

What is a healthy adult BMI?

Under the WHO international classification, the usual healthy range is 18.5 to 24.9. Other guidance may use lower action thresholds for some populations.

Why is Asian health-risk guidance available?

Some health authorities use action points of 23 and 27.5 because cardiometabolic risk may occur at a lower BMI in several Asian and other higher-risk ethnic backgrounds.

Does country automatically determine the BMI standard?

No. Country changes local context only. Choose the reference standard deliberately because nationality and individual health risk are not the same thing.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Highly muscular people may receive a high BMI even when body fat is not high. Waist measures and professional body-composition assessment may add context.

Can this calculator be used during pregnancy?

Current pregnancy weight should not be interpreted using a standard adult BMI result. Use pregnancy-specific clinical guidance.

Can children use this calculator?

No. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age growth references rather than adult thresholds.

What is waist-to-height ratio?

It divides waist circumference by height. A ratio below 0.5 is a commonly used practical target, while higher values may indicate increased central adiposity.

Are any live rates required?

No. BMI uses a fixed formula and published health-reference thresholds. There are no currency, market or interest rates in the calculation.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. The result is an educational screening estimate. A healthcare professional can consider medical history, body composition, blood pressure and other relevant findings.
