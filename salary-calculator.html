What is gross salary?

Gross salary is total earnings before income tax, retirement contributions, social insurance and other payroll deductions.

What is take-home pay?

Take-home pay, or net pay, is the amount remaining after employee taxes and deductions are subtracted.

Why can the estimate differ from my payslip?

Employers may use year-to-date income, exact contribution schedules, tax credits, benefits, payroll rounding and adjustments not entered here.

How are Malaysian EPF, SOCSO and EIS handled?

The Malaysia preset estimates EPF, SOCSO and EIS using current published rates and ceilings. Official wage-band tables can differ slightly from percentage estimates.

Does changing currency change the tax calculation?

No. Payroll is calculated in the selected country’s base currency first, then converted for display using the latest available exchange rate.

Can I calculate the gross salary needed for a target net pay?

Yes. Select Net to Gross. The calculator uses an iterative search because progressive taxes prevent a simple one-step formula.

Are exchange rates live?

Currency conversion requests the latest available working-day rate from the Frankfurter service and displays the update date.

Is this a payslip or tax return?

No. It is an educational estimate for planning and salary comparison. Use official payroll software for compliance.

7. Formula & Method

Take-home pay = gross earnings − income tax −
employee contributions − other deductions
  • Gross earnings include salary, overtime, bonus and taxable allowances.
  • Income tax uses the selected country preset and entered deductions.
  • Employee contributions include retirement and statutory programmes.
  • Employer cost includes employer contributions and benefits.
