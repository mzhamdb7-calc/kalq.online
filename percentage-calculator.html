Identify the Base Value

Confirm which number represents the whole. Most incorrect results come from dividing by the wrong reference value.

Separate Percent and Percentage Points

A move from 20% to 25% is 5 percentage points, but it is a 25% relative increase.

Reverse Changes Carefully

Undoing a 20% increase requires division by 1.20; subtracting 20% from the final value does not recover the original.

Keep Full Precision

Round only the displayed answer. Intermediate rounding can create noticeable differences in discounts, taxes and repeated changes.

Percentage of a Number

Part = Number × Percentage ÷ 100
Example: 800 × 25 ÷ 100 = 200.

What Percentage?

Percentage = Part ÷ Total × 100
The total must not be zero.

Percentage Change

Change % = (New − Original) ÷ |Original| × 100
A positive result is an increase; a negative result is a decrease.

Reverse Percentage

Original = Final ÷ (1 ± Percentage ÷ 100)
Use plus after an increase and minus after a decrease.

Percentage of a number

15% of 240 = 36. Convert 15% to 0.15, then multiply by 240.

What percentage?

48 out of 60 = 80%. Divide 48 by 60, then multiply by 100.

Percentage increase

120 to 150 = 25% increase. The change is 30 and the original reference is 120.

Reverse a discount

$80 after 20% off came from $100. Divide 80 by 0.80.

Wrong reference value

Percentage change normally divides by the original value, not the new value.

Percent vs percentage points

A move from 40% to 45% is 5 percentage points but a 12.5% relative increase.

Adding successive changes

Two 10% decreases produce a 19% total decrease because the second change uses the reduced value.

Reversing by subtraction

Do not undo a 20% increase by subtracting 20% from the final value; divide by 1.20.

What is a percentage?

A percentage expresses a value as a share of 100. For example, 25% equals 25/100 or 0.25.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For example, 20% of 500 is 100.

How do I find what percentage one value is of another?

Divide the part by the total and multiply by 100. The total cannot be zero.

How is percentage change calculated?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the absolute original value and multiply by 100.

What if the original value is zero?

Ordinary percentage change is undefined because division by zero is impossible. The calculator reports the absolute change instead.

Can a percentage exceed 100%?

Yes. A value of 150% is one and a half times the reference value, and an increase can exceed 100%.

Are successive percentage changes added together?

Usually not. Each change applies to the latest value, so two 10% decreases produce a total reduction of 19%.

Does this calculator use live rates?

Yes. Percentage formulas remain fixed mathematics, while monetary input values are converted with the latest available working-day exchange rate when the currency changes.
