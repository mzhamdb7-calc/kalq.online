Course status treatment

Graded and eligible Repeated attempt rows can count toward GPA. Pass/fail, transfer, audit, withdrawn and incomplete rows are excluded by default and remain visible in the report.

Repeated-course treatment

Use a repeat group or course code plus attempt number. The selected policy can include both attempts, keep the latest, replace the earlier attempt or use the highest grade.

Semester GPA

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ Included Credit Hours

Courses with more credit hours have a larger influence. Pass/fail or audit courses should be excluded unless the institution explicitly assigns grade points.

Cumulative CGPA

Projected CGPA = [(Current CGPA × Completed Credits) + Current Quality Points] ÷ [Completed Credits + Current Credits]

This is more accurate than averaging semester GPAs when semesters contain different credit totals.

Required Future GPA

Required GPA = [Target × (Prior + Current + Future Credits) − Prior Quality Points − Current Quality Points] ÷ Future Credits

The current semester is included before the remaining future-credit window. A required value above the scale maximum means the target is not reachable within that window.

Useful planning tips

  • Confirm the exact institutional grade-point table.
  • Give priority to high-credit courses because they carry more weight.
  • Check repeated-course and failed-course policies before trusting projections.
  • Use scenarios rather than one optimistic grade assumption.

Common mistakes

  • Taking a simple average of letter grades.
  • Including pass/fail courses without grade points.
  • Using a generic percentage conversion.
  • Rounding individual courses too early.
  • Assuming all Malaysian universities use identical points.

GPA versus CGPA

GPA normally describes one semester or selected group of courses. CGPA combines all included academic work. A strong new semester can raise CGPA, but the change becomes smaller after many credits have already been completed.

What is GPA?

Grade Point Average is a credit-weighted measure of performance for a semester or selected group of courses.

What is CGPA?

Cumulative Grade Point Average combines quality points and included credits across all relevant semesters.

Can Malaysian students use this calculator?

Yes. Select an institutional preset or edit a custom scale. Malaysian universities may assign different points to grades such as A− and B+.

Why does my transcript show a different result?

Your institution may use different rounding, repeat-course, transfer-credit, failure or exclusion rules. Verify the selected policy against official regulations.

Do high-credit courses affect GPA more?

Yes. A four-credit course normally contributes twice as much as a two-credit course with the same grade point.

Are failed courses included?

Often yes, because a failing grade may carry zero quality points while its credits remain attempted. Policies vary, so use the inclusion switch and official rules.

How are repeated courses handled?

Institutions may replace the earlier grade, count both attempts or keep the latest attempt. Select the matching policy for your planning notes.

Can a percentage be converted directly to GPA?

Only when the selected institution publishes an official marks-to-grade table. Generic conversions can be misleading.

What is a weighted GPA?

A weighted GPA adds extra points for advanced courses. This is common in some school systems but is not the default university CGPA method.

Can the calculator predict future CGPA?

Yes. Enter current CGPA, completed credits, future credits and a target. The projection remains an estimate based on the assumptions entered.

Why did CGPA move only slightly?

After many completed credits, a single semester forms a smaller share of the cumulative total, so CGPA changes more slowly.

Is this result official?

No. It is a planning estimate. Your institution’s official transcript and academic regulations are authoritative.
