Formula used

—

  • Gestational age is counted from the pregnancy start date used by the selected method.
  • A typical due date estimate is 280 days from the first day of the last menstrual period.
  • The clinician-provided due date takes priority when entered.

How to interpret the result

—

  • The date is an estimate, not a prediction of the exact birth day.
  • Early ultrasound can provide more reliable dating when the LMP is uncertain.
  • Multiple pregnancies require individualised clinical planning.

Using the calculator well

  • Use the first day of the last normal menstrual period, not the day bleeding ended.
  • Adjust cycle length only when your usual cycle is reliably different from 28 days.
  • For IVF, select the embryo age at transfer.
  • Keep the due date given by your maternity-care team as your primary medical date.

Planning safely

  • Arrange antenatal care early; Malaysia MOH prefers the booking visit before 12 weeks.
  • Bring scan reports and dating information to appointments.
  • Do not use this tool to assess symptoms, fetal growth or pregnancy complications.
  • Seek prompt medical care for concerning symptoms or urgent pregnancy concerns.
How is the estimated due date calculated?

The LMP method usually adds 280 days to the first day of the last menstrual period and adjusts for a cycle length that differs from 28 days.

Is the due date exact?

No. It is a planning estimate. A clinician may establish or revise the date using ultrasound, IVF information and the full clinical history.

Why does pregnancy dating begin before conception?

Gestational age is conventionally counted from the first day of the last menstrual period. Conception generally occurs later.

Can I use the calculator with irregular periods?

Yes, but LMP dating may be less reliable. Malaysian guidance recommends dating ultrasound when periods are irregular or the LMP is uncertain.

Which date should I use after IVF?

Use the embryo-transfer date and choose whether the embryo was three or five days old at transfer.

What is the difference between gestational age and fetal age?

Gestational age begins from the pregnancy start date used for obstetric dating. Estimated conception age is usually about two weeks less.

When should antenatal care begin in Malaysia?

The Malaysia Ministry of Health recommends the booking visit as soon as possible, preferably before 12 weeks.

Does this work for twins?

It can estimate gestational age and a basic due date, but multiple pregnancies need personalised monitoring and delivery planning.

Can this calculator diagnose a pregnancy problem?

No. It estimates dates and organises general planning information only.

Why is my scan date different from my LMP date?

Ovulation may have occurred earlier or later than assumed, cycles may vary, or the LMP may be uncertain. Your clinician decides which date should be used.
