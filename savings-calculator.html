Schedule deposits immediately after income is received. Consistency often matters more than trying to time interest-rate changes.

Increase Deposits Gradually

Compare Net Returns

Protect Near-Term Goals

For money needed soon, prioritise access, capital stability and deposit-protection eligibility instead of assuming a higher return is always better.

Future Value of Starting Balance

FV = P(1 + i)N, where P is the starting balance, i is the periodic rate and N is the number of periods.

Future Value of Deposits

FV = PMT × ((1 + i)N − 1) ÷ i. Beginning-of-period deposits receive one additional period of growth.

Real Value

Real value = nominal value ÷ (1 + inflation)years.

Detailed Simulation

This calculator simulates each month so contribution increases, tax, fees, extra deposits and withdrawals can be included consistently.

What does this savings calculator estimate?

It estimates the future balance, total deposits, net earnings, inflation-adjusted value, goal progress and a monthly or annual savings schedule.

Which rate should I enter?

Use the annual interest or profit rate published for the actual account. The latest-published country deposit rate is only a broad reference and may not match a specific bank product.

What is the difference between interest and APY?

The entered annual rate is treated as a nominal rate. APY reflects the effect of the selected compounding frequency over one year.

Does contribution timing matter?

Yes. Beginning-of-period deposits receive one extra month of growth compared with end-of-period deposits.

How is inflation used?

The calculator discounts the future nominal balance using the entered annual inflation rate to estimate purchasing power in today’s money.

Can I use this for Islamic savings?

Yes. Select an Islamic account to change the wording from interest to profit. Actual product rules may differ, so confirm the bank’s published terms.

Are the displayed rates live?

FX data is retrieved from Frankfurter when available. Deposit and inflation figures are the latest published World Bank observations and include their data year; they are not real-time bank offers.

Is Malaysian deposit protection included?

The calculator can show a general PIDM reminder when MYR is selected and the projected eligible balance exceeds RM250,000. Confirm product eligibility and current limits directly with PIDM.

Why can my bank’s result differ?

Banks may use daily balances, tiered rates, promotional conditions, exact posting dates, product-specific tax treatment and different rounding methods.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an educational planning estimate and does not guarantee rates, returns, product eligibility or future purchasing power.
