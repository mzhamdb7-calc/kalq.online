What is a tip?

A tip or gratuity is an optional amount added for service. It is different from tax and from a service charge imposed by a business.

Should I tip before or after tax?

Many diners use the pre-tax subtotal, but local practice varies. Select the basis that matches your preference and receipt.

Is a service charge the same as a tip?

No. A service charge is added by the business. A tip is usually chosen by the customer. Check whether either amount is already included.

Is tipping required in Malaysia?

Tipping is generally optional. Rounding up or leaving a small gratuity may be appreciated, but check for an included service charge first.

How does custom splitting work?

Enter each person’s bill share. Tax, service charge, tip and rounding are then allocated proportionally to those shares.

Why is the effective tip rate different?

Discounts, the selected tip basis and rounding can make the tip a different percentage of the original subtotal.

Are exchange rates live?

Optional conversion uses the latest available working-day reference rate. Your bank, card network or wallet may use a different rate or add fees.

Can I enter a fixed tip?

Yes. Choose Fixed amount under Optional Input. The displayed effective rate will be calculated automatically.
