About KALQ

KALQ provides free calculators, converters and educational tools for everyday decisions.

What KALQ provides

The site includes focused tools for finance, mathematics, health, education and everyday planning. It is intended for people who want a quick estimate followed by enough context to understand the result.

Formula and source selection

Formulas are selected from established mathematical methods and authoritative sources where relevant. Calculator pages identify assumptions, limitations and references when those details materially affect interpretation.

Testing and error reporting

Calculators are reviewed with representative inputs, boundary cases and independent hand calculations. Automated and manual checks reduce errors, but no calculator can cover every situation. Use the Contact page instructions to report a calculation problem.

Privacy approach

Calculator inputs are designed to be processed in the browser. Theme and tool preferences may use local browser storage. KALQ does not currently include advertising or analytics code in this package.

Limitations

Results are estimates for informational and educational use. Rules, rates, personal circumstances and specialist methods can differ, so important decisions should be checked with a qualified adviser.
