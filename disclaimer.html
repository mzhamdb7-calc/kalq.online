Calculator Disclaimer

KALQ results are estimates for informational and educational purposes.

General calculators

Results depend on the values, units and assumptions entered. Rounding and simplified formulas may produce differences from other tools.

Financial and investment calculators

Projections do not promise returns and may not include every fee, tax, inflation effect or market risk. They are not financial advice.

Tax calculators

Tax rules depend on jurisdiction and effective period and may change. Confirm important results with current official guidance or a qualified tax specialist.

Health calculators

Health results are screening or planning estimates, not diagnoses. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare provider for personal medical decisions.

Education calculators

Institutions may use different grade scales, exclusions and rounding rules. Confirm the applicable academic policy.

Construction calculators

Material estimates can require waste allowances and specialist measurement. Local codes, product specifications and site conditions may differ.

Live rates and external data

A calculator must identify its source and update time before a value is described as live. Otherwise, rates are user-entered assumptions.
