What does the Outdoor Weather Calculator estimate?

Estimate outdoor suitability from sunshine, wind, rain, heat and UV forecasts.

Is this an official warning?

No. It is a planning estimate. Confirm current information with local weather, health, transport or emergency authorities.

Why can the result change?

Forecast models and local conditions update over time. Refresh the calculation closer to the planned activity.

Can nearby locations differ?

Yes. Weather, pollution, terrain and local infrastructure can vary over short distances.

Can I test my own scenario?

Yes. Use the optional inputs to compare a manual scenario with the available forecast.

What measurement system is used?

The page uses metric weather units and converts forecast variables into a 0–100 planning score.
