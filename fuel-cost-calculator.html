Fuel Required

Fuel = total distance × consumption ÷ 100

Fuel Cost

Fuel cost = fuel required × price per litre

Total Journey Cost

Total = fuel + tolls + parking + other expenses

Cost per Passenger

Passenger share = total journey cost ÷ passengers

Monthly Projection

Monthly = one-journey cost × trips per week × 52 ÷ 12

Emissions Estimate

CO₂ estimate = fuel used × selected emission factor. This is a tailpipe estimate, not a full lifecycle assessment.

How is fuel cost calculated?

The calculator estimates litres used from total distance and fuel consumption, then multiplies those litres by the selected price per litre.

Does the calculator use live fuel prices?

It requests the newest available official or trusted rate for supported markets and displays the source and effective date. You can always replace it with a local pump price.

Does it support Malaysian fuel prices?

Yes. The Malaysia option reads RON95, RON97, BUDI 95, SKPS and regional diesel fields from the official data.gov.my fuel-price dataset when available.

Why can my actual cost be different?

Traffic, idling, gradients, speed, weather, tyre pressure, air-conditioning, vehicle load and station pricing can all change the real result.

Should I use one-way or round trip?

Select round trip when the return distance is approximately the same. For a different return route, enter the combined distance as a one-way calculation.

Can I include tolls and parking?

Yes. Optional costs are added separately so the results show both fuel-only cost and the complete journey budget.

How is cost per passenger calculated?

The complete journey cost is divided equally by the entered passenger count. Adjust the number if only some occupants are sharing the cost.

Can I estimate commuting costs?

Enter trips per week to generate weekly, monthly and annual recurring-cost projections.

What fuel consumption figure should I enter?

Use your vehicle’s recent average in litres per 100 kilometres. A real-world average over several trips is usually the most practical figure.

What happens when a live provider is unavailable?

The calculator keeps the latest entered value, marks it as manual or cached, and continues to work. Refresh later or type the local price directly.

Are currency conversions exact retail rates?

No. Reference exchange rates can differ from bank, card-network and cash-exchange rates. The applied source and date are shown in the rate status.

Is the emissions figure official?

No. It is an estimate based on litres consumed and a configurable tailpipe factor. Vehicle technology and fuel blend can affect the actual value.
