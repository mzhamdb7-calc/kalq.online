Weighted Monsoon Score

Monsoon score = Σ (factor score × factor weight). The dedicated model weights prolonged rainfall, wet-hour persistence, drainage saturation, flood potential, monsoon wind, visibility and warnings.

Monsoon-only Data

Hourly rain and showers, precipitation hours, prior 48-hour accumulation, soil moisture, humidity, sustained wind and gusts are loaded separately from storm data.

Probability Conversion

P = 100 ÷ (1 + e−k(S−b)), where S is the monsoon-specific weighted score.

Confidence

Confidence decreases with forecast horizon and missing soil data, and increases when complete hourly data and an official alert source are available.

What does the Monsoon Calculator estimate?

It estimates the likelihood of normal opening, a delay, remote learning or closure using weather severity, timing, travel exposure and local preparedness.

Is the result an official closure announcement?

No. It is a planning estimate. Confirm the final status with the school, organisation or relevant authority.

Does it use live weather?

It requests the latest available hourly forecast when the page can reach the weather service. The exact retrieval time and source are shown on the page and report.

Why do wet hours and prior rainfall matter?

Monsoon disruption is shaped by how long rain persists and whether earlier rainfall has already saturated soil, drains and low-lying routes.

How is this different from the Storm Calculator?

This page models prolonged rain, prior 48-hour accumulation, soil moisture and drainage pressure. It does not reuse the storm page’s CAPE, lightning or convective-gust score.

Why can nearby locations receive different results?

Elevation, terrain, forecast intensity, road treatment, drainage, transport routes and organisational policy may differ over short distances.

What does forecast confidence mean?

It describes the reliability of the estimate based on forecast horizon, data completeness and alert availability—not a guarantee that the outcome will occur.

Can I test my own scenario?

Yes. Turn on the manual weather override under Optional Inputs and enter custom rain, flood, wind and visibility values.

Does a weather warning guarantee closure?

No. A warning raises the risk score, but the organisation or authority still makes the operational decision.

What measurement system is used?

The calculator uses SI units: degrees Celsius, millimetres of rain, kilometres per hour and kilometres of visibility.
