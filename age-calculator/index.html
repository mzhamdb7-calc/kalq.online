Formula and calculation methodThe calculator uses calendar-aware date arithmetic. It does not treat every month as 30 days or every year as 365 days.
Completed years
Reference year − Birth year − birthday adjustment
Exact period
Birth date + complete years + complete months + remaining days
Total days
Reference date ordinal − Birth date ordinal
Birthday progress
Days since last birthday ÷ days in current birthday year × 100
Calendar ruleComplete units only

A year or month is counted only after its corresponding anniversary date has been reached.

People born on February 29 can select the preferred non-leap-year anniversary convention.

Time unitsHours and minutes are approximate

Zodiac and Chinese calendar

Western zodiac uses standard tropical date ranges. Chinese zodiac uses the browser’s Chinese-calendar year, so the animal changes at the Chinese New Year rather than January 1.

Stone, flower and season

Birthstones and flowers are month-based traditions, with recognised alternatives shown where applicable. Seasons use meteorological months for both hemispheres.

Generation label

The displayed label uses a stated common range. Generational boundaries are broad social reference points and are not universally agreed.

Core formulas

Years = reference year − birth year − birthday adjustment
Total days = reference ordinal − birth ordinal
Progress = days since birthday ÷ birthday-year days

Calculation conventions

  • Calendar units are completed sequentially.
  • Month lengths and leap years are respected.
  • Date arithmetic is performed as calendar days, avoiding daylight-saving distortion.
  • Hours and minutes equal complete days multiplied by 24 and 1,440.
  • Historical dates use the proleptic Gregorian calendar.
Validation meaning: The component calculation and total-day calculation are internally consistent. This does not make the report a legal certificate.
