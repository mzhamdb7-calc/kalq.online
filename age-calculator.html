How the calculation works

The calculator counts completed calendar years first, then completed months, followed by remaining days. Named time zones are used for elapsed hours, minutes and seconds, including daylight-saving changes.

  • Use an unambiguous date picker.
  • Select the calculation date for past or future age.
  • Add actual times and select the correct time zone when hour-level precision is needed.
  • Choose a leap-day policy for 29 February birthdays.

Useful tips

  • Check that the birth date is earlier than the calculation date.
  • Use the region selector for familiar date formatting.
  • Legal age rules vary by country and purpose.
  • The selected Hijri method is shown beside the result; local moon sightings can still differ by one day.
How is exact age calculated?

Completed calendar years, months and days are counted between the birth date and calculation date.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the calculation date to any date on or after the birth date.

Why can total days differ from calendar age?

Calendar months and years have unequal lengths, while total days measures continuous elapsed time.

How are leap-day birthdays handled?

Choose whether 29 February is observed on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years.

Why can Hijri dates vary?

Regional moon sightings and calendar algorithms can produce a one-day difference.

Is the Chinese zodiac exact near Lunar New Year?

The calculator reads the Chinese calendar year for the exact birth date, so January and February birthdays follow the Lunar New Year boundary when browser support is available.

Can this determine legal adulthood?

It calculates chronological age only. Legal thresholds differ by jurisdiction and situation.
