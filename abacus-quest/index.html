Formula and scoringThe displayed value and performance measures use transparent formulas. Learning mastery is kept separate from the entertainment score.
Rod value
(upper bead × 5 + active lower beads) × place value
Total value
Σ [(upper bead × 5 + lower beads) × 10ⁿ]
Complement to 5
1 ↔ 4  ·  2 ↔ 3
Complement to 10
1 ↔ 9  ·  2 ↔ 8  ·  3 ↔ 7  ·  4 ↔ 6  ·  5 ↔ 5
Question score
(100 + speed bonus + streak bonus) × difficulty multiplier − hint penalty
Mastery
40% accuracy + 25% speed + 20% consistency + 15% difficulty
Speed bonusUp to 50
Streak bonus10 × streak, max 100
Hint penalty25 points
Beginner multiplier1.00
Expert multiplier2.00
Soroban example: 4,382 = 4 × 1,000 + 3 × 100 + 8 × 10 + 2. Scoring example: A correct Medium answer with a 35-point speed bonus and a five-answer streak scores (100 + 35 + 50) × 1.35 = 250 points after rounding. A hint reduces the result by 25 points.

Tips and common mistakesThese practical checks improve skill without adding more setup fields or interrupting the active game.

Training tips

  • Learn place value before starting timed challenges.
  • Reset the Soroban to zero before every new question.
  • Practise complements to five before complements to ten.
  • Prioritise accuracy before increasing speed.
  • Use short daily sessions instead of one very long session.
  • Review incorrect answers before raising the difficulty.
  • Say the intermediate value mentally while moving beads.
  • Start Flash Anzan after normal movements feel automatic.

Common mistakes

  • Counting beads that are not touching the reckoning bar.
  • Entering a value on the wrong place-value rod.
  • Forgetting to reset a bead from the previous question.
  • Missing a carry or borrow between rods.
  • Moving only one lower bead when several should be active.
  • Chasing speed before a movement pattern is reliable.
  • Using a hint but overlooking the complement relationship.
  • Treating a game score as the same measure as mastery.
