Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

The current KalQ package does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Essential storage

Browser local storage may remember theme, language and tool preferences so they remain available on the same device.

Calculator history

Some tools may keep recent inputs or results locally when that feature is clearly shown. Local data can be cleared through the tool or browser controls.

Analytics and advertising cookies

No analytics or advertising scripts are included in this package. If they are introduced, KalQ must identify the provider, purpose, retention and consent choices before activation.

Third-party content

Opening an external reference takes you to another site, which may use its own cookies under its own policy.

Consent

A consent banner is not displayed while non-essential tracking is inactive. Future non-essential cookies must not load before any legally required choice is recorded.

Browser controls

Browsers allow you to inspect, block and delete cookies or local storage. Blocking storage may prevent preferences from being remembered.

Cookie settings

There is currently no non-essential cookie preference to change. This section is the destination for the footer Cookie Settings link and will be updated if consent-managed services are added.
