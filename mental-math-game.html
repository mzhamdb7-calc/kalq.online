How the round works

  • Choose a mode, difficulty, duration and question type.
  • Enter an answer and press Enter or use the on-screen keypad.
  • Correct streaks increase the score multiplier.
  • Automatic difficulty raises or lowers the challenge based on recent performance.

Score calculation

Each correct answer earns a difficulty score plus a speed bonus. A streak multiplier increases every five consecutive correct answers. Incorrect and skipped answers break the streak but do not create a negative score.

Faster calculation strategies

  • Break numbers into tens and ones before adding or subtracting.
  • Use doubling and halving for multiplication, such as 16 × 25 = 8 × 50.
  • Estimate first to catch answers with the wrong size or sign.
  • Convert common percentages: 10%, 25%, 50% and 75%.

Practice effectively

  • Prioritize accuracy before speed.
  • Use Practice Mode for weak operations.
  • Repeat short sessions instead of one exhausting session.
  • Review mistakes and solve similar questions immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does automatic difficulty work?

The level rises after sustained correct answers and falls slightly after repeated mistakes, keeping questions challenging without becoming frustrating.

Can I use fractions or decimals?

Yes. Enable them in Customize Game. Answers may be entered as decimals; generated fraction questions are designed to produce simple values.

Is my score stored online?

No account is required. The personal best is stored locally in your browser on this device.
