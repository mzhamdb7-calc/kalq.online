Pay More Than the Minimum

A fixed payment that stays above the declining minimum usually clears the balance faster and reduces total interest.

Stop Adding New Purchases

Continuing to spend can delay the payoff date or prevent the balance from falling, especially when the payment is small.

Use Windfalls Strategically

A bonus, refund or other one-time payment reduces the balance immediately and lowers later finance charges.

Confirm Your Statement Rate

Use the APR or finance-charge rate on your latest statement. Promotional, purchase and cash-advance balances may use different rates.

Monthly Interest

Monthly rate = APR ÷ 12
Estimated interest = interest-bearing balance × monthly rate.

Ending Balance

What does a credit card payoff calculator show?

It estimates the payment, payoff date, total interest, fees and repayment schedule under the assumptions you enter.

Which interest rate should I enter?

Use the purchase APR or finance-charge rate on your latest statement. Use a separate calculation if part of the balance has another rate.

Why can the result differ from my statement?

Issuers may use daily balances, exact posting dates, grace-period rules, transaction-specific rates and rounding methods that a general calculator cannot fully reproduce.

What happens if I pay only the minimum?

The required payment usually falls as the balance falls, which can extend repayment and increase total interest. Issuer rules may also add instalments and overdue amounts.

Can I include continued card spending?

Yes. Enter expected monthly purchases under Advanced options. The calculator will warn when purchases, fees and interest prevent the balance from declining.

How does the Malaysian preset work?

It loads a common published finance-charge tier of 15%, 17% or 18% per year. Always confirm your actual rate and minimum-payment rule on the latest statement.

Are currency rates live?

Currency conversions use the latest available working-day reference rate from the Frankfurter exchange-rate service. The rate and update date are displayed beside the currency control.

Can I calculate the payment for a specific payoff date?

Yes. Select Target payoff date, choose a future date, and the calculator will estimate the monthly payment required under the entered assumptions.

Where are saved plans stored?

Saved plans are stored locally in your browser on this device. They are not uploaded, and clearing browser storage may remove them.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is an educational planning estimate. Contact your issuer or a qualified adviser when you need account-specific guidance.

6. Formula & Method

Monthly balance update:
Bt+1 = (Bt + It + Ft + Nt) − Pt
where:
Bt = Starting balance
It = Interest for the month
Ft = Fees
Nt = New purchases
Pt = Payment made
