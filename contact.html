Current contact status

To avoid presenting a form that cannot send messages, this page does not include a Send button. The site owner must add a verified KalQ contact address or a configured external form endpoint before public messages can be accepted.

Report a calculator error

When a channel is configured, include the calculator name, the inputs used, the result shown, the result you expected and the source supporting the correction. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Suggest a calculator

Describe the decision the tool should support, the required inputs, expected outputs, country or jurisdiction, and any authoritative formula source.

Technical and privacy questions

Useful reports include the page address, device and browser, the action attempted and a short description of the problem. Privacy questions should identify the relevant policy section without sharing confidential data.

General feedback

Feedback about clarity, accessibility, mobile use and report quality is welcome after a working contact channel is added.
