Privacy Policy

This policy explains the privacy behaviour of the current static KalQ website package.

Calculator inputs

Calculator inputs and calculations are intended to run locally in the browser. Do not enter confidential or identifying information unless a calculator clearly requires it.

Local storage and essential preferences

The site may use browser local storage for theme, language, recent-tool or calculator preferences. This information remains on the device unless browser settings, extensions or synchronisation features handle it differently.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This package does not include Google Analytics, Google AdSense or other advertising scripts. No advertising-cookie consent banner is shown because non-essential tracking is not active. If analytics or advertising is added later, this policy and consent handling must be updated first.

Contact messages and retention

No contact form endpoint is currently configured. If a contact channel is added, its provider, retention approach and data use must be documented here.

Third-party services and external links

Reference links may lead to external organisations with their own privacy practices. KalQ does not control those sites.

User choices and policy updates

Users can clear local storage and cookies through browser controls. Material policy changes will be published on this page with a revised date.
