Lump-Sum Growth

FV = PV(1 + r/n)nt
PV is the starting amount, r is the nominal annual rate, n is compounding periods per year and t is years.

Regular Contributions

FV = PMT × [((1 + i)N − 1) ÷ i]
For evenly spaced deposits, this is the standard annuity formula. This calculator instead schedules deposits on their exact calendar dates and applies an equivalent daily growth rate.

Effective Annual Rate

EAR = (1 + r/n)n − 1
Continuous compounding uses EAR = er − 1.

Inflation-Adjusted Value

Real value = nominal value ÷ (1 + inflation)t
This estimates purchasing power in today’s money.

What does this compound interest calculator show?

It estimates future balance, contributions, interest, effective annual rate, purchasing power, costs, milestones, scenarios and an annual schedule.

Which annual rate should I use?

Use the contractual rate for a savings product or a reasonable expected return for an investment. A policy or deposit reference rate is not a guaranteed return.

Are the displayed rates live?

Currency conversion uses the latest available working-day rate. Published benchmark and inflation observations are displayed separately; they affect the calculation only when you explicitly apply them.

What does compounding frequency mean?

It is how often earned interest is added to the balance. More frequent compounding slightly increases the effective annual rate when the nominal rate is unchanged.

When are regular contributions added?

Select beginning or end of period. Beginning deposits receive one additional period of growth compared with otherwise identical end-of-period deposits.

Why can actual investment returns differ?

Market returns, product rates, contribution dates, fees, taxes, rounding and withdrawals may differ from the assumptions used in the projection.

How is inflation handled?

The calculator discounts the projected nominal balance using the entered annual inflation rate to estimate value in today’s purchasing power.

Are fees and taxes compounded?

Annual fees are prorated daily. Estimated tax is applied to positive interest as it accrues, reducing the amount that remains invested.

Can I include a one-time deposit?

Yes. Enter the amount and the month in which it should be added. It appears separately in the detailed schedule and report.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an educational planning estimate. Confirm product terms and obtain qualified advice for important financial decisions.

10. Formula & Method / Notes

Formula (With Contributions)
FV = P(1 + r)t + C × (1 + r)t − 1r × (1 + r)
Where:
FV = Future Value
P = Starting Amount
C = Periodic Contribution
r = Periodic Rate
t = Total Number of Periods
Notes & Limitations
  • Calculations use exact-date daily accrual.
  • Returns are estimates and not guaranteed.
  • Future taxes may vary.
  • Inflation is assumed constant.
  • Results change with assumptions and timing.
