Numerical method

What is a polynomial root?

A root is a value that makes the polynomial equal to zero. It is also called a zero or solution.

Can this calculator find complex roots?

Yes. It calculates real and non-real complex roots and shows every root on a complex-plane graph.

What does multiplicity mean?

Multiplicity is the number of times a root occurs. A repeated root may change how the polynomial touches or crosses the x-axis.

Why is the residual important?

The residual is |f(r)| after substituting a calculated root. A value very close to zero indicates a reliable numerical result.

Can I enter a factored polynomial?

Yes. Inputs such as (x − 2)(x + 1)^2 are expanded automatically by the built-in parser.

Why do some decimals differ between calculators?

Different tools may use different precision, stopping criteria and rounding. Compare the residual rather than only the displayed digits.

Does this calculator need live rates?

No external rate applies to polynomial roots. “Live” means results and visualizations update automatically when the input changes.
